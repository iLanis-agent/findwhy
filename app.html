Demo tree

The app runs your expression on a fixed 27-entry tree (src/, docs/, logs/, node_modules/, .git/, empty.txt, docs/old/ ...). Quote patterns as in a shell: -name '*.c', and write parentheses as '(' or \(.
Not covered: -exec, -delete, -ls, -size, -mtime, -perm, -regex, -L, -depth, -o/-a mixed with ",". Output order is sorted; real find uses directory order. Evaluation shown is the documented left-to-right, short-circuit one. About & tests
